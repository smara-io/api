Frequently asked questions

Can I try before I buy?
Absolutely. The free tier gives you 10,000 memories, full API access, semantic search, and Ebbinghaus decay — no credit card required. Most solo developers never need to upgrade.
What happens if I exceed my memory limit?
We use graceful degradation, not hard cutoffs. You'll get a warning at 90% usage, and new writes will queue for review rather than being rejected. Existing memories remain fully searchable. Upgrade anytime to lift the limit instantly.
Can I switch plans?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes are prorated — you'll only pay for what you use. Downgrading preserves your existing memories; you just won't be able to create new ones beyond the lower tier's limit.
Is my data private?
Yes. Each API key isolates your data in its own schema. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest. We never use your memories for training or share them with third parties.
Do you offer annual billing?
Coming soon. Annual plans will save you 20% compared to monthly billing. Join the waitlist or start monthly — you'll be automatically migrated to annual pricing when it launches.
